# Scene 05 — Ever Crescendo

| | |
|---|---|
| **Letter lines** | L49–L61 |
| **Settings** | S3 Sinnett's study · "The faces of a million years" (vision montage) · S10 the endless arena · a mountain path (vision) |
| **Characters** | Alfred Percy Sinnett, Koot' Hoomi (V.O., one silhouette), Helena Petrovna Blavatsky (vision). Crowds, no individual figures |
| **Feeling** | Dread, vertigo. **The peak** |
| **Weight** | L (the longest scene) |

## Purpose

- This is the scene the viewer remembers. The letter's central argument is turned into one rising sequence: each marvel demands a bigger one, the audience outgrows any stage, and the anger falls on the people who can be reached.
- Answer Sinnett's test fully. If the Masters consented, success itself would become the danger.
- End the peak in silence, then give the letter's answer to Sinnett's complaint about secrecy: truth is "conquered step by step, not at a rush."

**Rules for this scene.** K.H. lines are word for word with line numbers, and "…" marks a cut. Sinnett has one [dramatized] line, which voices "your daily remark" that the letter quotes back to him (L54). The arena is a single imagined space, the "supposing we consented" of L50, and everything in it is hypothetical. The crowd's anger and the educated classes' scorn are staged as the letter describes them, without taking sides.

## Beat outline

1. **A million years.** The same human gesture across the ages: a hand closing around what it has, and a face turning away from something new.
2. **Supposing we consented.** The study becomes a small theatre. Sinnett and Blavatsky on stage, a handful of Simla friends in the seats. The first marvel: the cup.
3. **Ever crescendo.** Each marvel must top the last: the cup, then the brooch, then the newspaper, then things stranger still. The applause grows, and so does the theatre. Walls fall back, the audience multiplies.
4. **The eye-witness.** Sinnett's own remark ("one cannot be expected to believe unless he becomes an eye-witness") echoes from the stage.
5. **A lifetime.** The tests never end. On stage, Sinnett ages: his beard greys, his shoulders bend.
6. **Hundreds of millions.** The arena reaches the horizon, and beyond its walls a sea of people who can never see. They press against the walls.
7. **The visible agents.** High above, behind a curtain, the silhouette of the "invisible operators". The crowd can't reach it. The walls break, and the crowd turns on the people it can reach: Blavatsky, alone in the spotlight.
8. **Shreds.** In the boxes, the educated classes tear their newspapers into shreds that fall like snow over the stage. This is the top of the peak.
9. **Silence.** The arena empties. Paper drifts down onto a bare stage.
10. **Secrecy.** Back in the study, Sinnett, shaken. "You blame us for our great secrecy."
11. **Step by step.** A mountain path climbing into cloud, cut step by step into the rock. A single footprint on the first step.

## Script

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — DAY

The cloud from the end of scene 04 still over the sun. The room
grey. Sinnett, very still, the letter held close.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     As for human nature in general, it is the same now as
     it was a million of years ago:                        [L49]
```

```
VISION — THE FACES OF A MILLION YEARS

A rapid montage, each shot the same composition, the same
gesture:

— by firelight in a cave, a HAND closes around a flint knife;
  a face turns from a stranger at the cave mouth.
— in a walled medieval town, a HAND closes the shutter; a face
  turns from a preacher in the square.
— in a counting-house, a HAND closes a ledger; a face turns
  from a man with a pamphlet.
— on a Victorian street, a HAND closes a front door; a face
  turns from a figure holding something that glows faintly blue.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …Prejudice based upon selfishness; a general
     unwillingness to give up an established order of things
     for new modes of life and thought — and occult study
     requires all that and much more — ; pride and stubborn
     resistance to Truth if it but upsets their previous
     notions of things, — such are the characteristics of
     your age, and especially of the middle and lower
     classes.                                              [L49]

The last door closes. Black.
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: A SMALL THEATRE

A pin-spot of light. A little wooden STAGE, the size of the
study, red velvet curtains. Eight or nine seats.

In the seats: the PICNIC GUESTS from scene 01, the faithful of
Simla, leaning forward, eager.

On stage: SINNETT, younger than we have seen him (darker beard,
upright, eager), in his frock coat, like a master of
ceremonies. Beside him, BLAVATSKY in black, seated, smoking.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     What then would be the results of the most astounding
     phenomena, supposing we consented to have them
     produced?                                             [L50]

Sinnett gestures. Blavatsky lifts one hand.

From nowhere, the dug-up CUP appears on a pedestal, earth in
its rim. The little audience gasps. Applause, small and warm.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     However successful, danger would be growing
     proportionately with success.                         [L51]

Under the applause, the stage CREAKS.
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: CRESCENDO

The applause stops expectantly. The audience wants more.

Blavatsky lifts her hand again. The BROOCH appears, glittering,
in mid-air.

Bigger applause. The walls of the theatre slide back. More rows
of seats appear behind the first, filling with people.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     No choice would soon remain but to go on, ever
     crescendo, or to fall in this endless struggle with
     prejudice and ignorance killed by your own weapons.   [L52]

Again. A NEWSPAPER materialises in Sinnett's hand, a London
paper, impossibly here. He holds it high. A roar. The theatre
becomes an AMPHITHEATRE, tiers rising into darkness.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     Test after test would be required and would have to be
     furnished; every subsequent phenomenon expected to be
     more marvellous than the preceding one.               [L53]

Faster now. Each marvel bigger, stranger, quicker:
— a whole TREE grows from the stage in seconds;
— the pedestal LIFTS into the air;
— the blue AKÁS light floods the arena (the colour from scene
  03), and for one moment the whole crowd is lit with wonder.

And every time, the applause is shorter and the demand comes
quicker. Faces in the crowd: no longer amazed. Impatient.
Hungry. "Again."

Blavatsky's hands are shaking. She lights a new cigarette from
the old one.
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: THE EYE-WITNESS

Sinnett at the front of the stage, calling out to the crowd,
confident, the editor at his most persuasive.

                    SINNETT
          (to the crowd) [dramatized, echoes L54]
     One cannot be expected to believe unless he becomes an
     eye-witness!

                    K.H. (V.O.)
          (quietly, over the noise)
     Your daily remark is, that one cannot be expected to
     believe unless he becomes an eye-witness.             [L54]
     Would the lifetime of a man suffice to satisfy the
     whole world of skeptics?                              [L55]
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: A LIFETIME

A MONTAGE on stage, the same framing each time, the lights
changing like seasons:
— Sinnett presents a marvel. His beard darkens to grey.
— Sinnett presents a marvel. His hairline recedes.
— Sinnett presents a marvel. His shoulders bend. His voice,
  unheard now, is hoarse.

Until he stands as we know him from his portrait: bald crown,
full grey beard, older than his years, still holding up some
new wonder to a crowd that will not be satisfied.
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS

The camera RISES. The amphitheatre is now a vast ARENA, its
tiers stretching to the horizon, packed with faces.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     It may be an easy matter to increase the original number
     of believers at Simla to hundreds and thousands.      [L56]

The camera keeps rising, above the rim of the arena.

Outside its walls, as far as the eye can see, in every
direction: PEOPLE. A dark sea of them, millions upon millions,
in the plains, along rivers, in cities to the edge of the
world. None can see the stage. They see only the glow above
the walls, and hear the roar.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     But what of the hundreds of millions of those who could
     not be made eye-witnesses?                            [L57]

The sea of people begins to move. Toward the walls. A low
rumble, growing.
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: THE VISIBLE AGENTS

High above the stage, behind a vast dark CURTAIN, the faintest
SILHOUETTE of a figure: long hair, a fur cap, still and watching.
The invisible operator. Out of all reach.

The crowd outside hits the walls. Faces at the top of the wall,
shouting, fists raised: frightened, furious at what they cannot
understand.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     The ignorant — unable to grapple with the invisible
     operators —                                           [L58]

Their eyes go up to the curtain, to the silhouette. They can't
reach it.

Their eyes come down. To the stage. To the people who can be
seen.

The WALLS BREAK.

The crowd pours in, down the tiers, toward the light.

On the stage, in a single hard SPOTLIGHT: BLAVATSKY, alone.
Sinnett has been swept aside into the dark. She rises from her
chair, cigarette falling, as the roar comes down on her.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …might some day vent their rage on the visible agents
     at work;                                              [L58]
```

```
VISION — THE ARENA: SHREDS

In the high gilded BOXES around the arena, above the tumult:
GENTLEMEN AND LADIES in evening dress. Composed, amused,
disbelieving. They hold newspapers, THE PIONEER among them.

They tear them. Slowly. Neatly. Again and again.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …the higher and educated classes would go on
     disbelieving as ever, tearing you to shreds as before.  [L58]

The SHREDS fall from every box at once, thousands of white
scraps drifting down through the spotlight like snow, onto the
crowd, the stage, Blavatsky's upturned face.

Then everything stops.

SILENCE.

The roar is gone. The crowd is gone. The arena is empty to the
horizon.

Only the shreds, still falling, settling on a bare stage, an
empty chair and a cigarette burning out on the boards.

HOLD.
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — DAY

Sinnett, exactly as we left him. He hasn't moved. His hand on
the letter is not quite steady.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
          (gently)
     In common with many, you blame us for our great
     secrecy.                                              [L59]

Sinnett closes his eyes.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     Yet we know something of human nature for the
     experience of long centuries — aye, ages — has taught
     us.                                                   [L60]
```

```
VISION — THE MOUNTAIN PATH

Snow light. A long PATH climbs a mountainside into cloud. It is
made of STEPS cut into the rock, thousands of them, each one
shallow, each one made by hand.

In silence, one more step is being cut: a chisel, a hand, the
fur-trimmed sleeve we saw in scene 02. Chips of stone fall.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     And, we know, that so long as science has anything to
     learn, and a shadow of religious dogmatism lingers in the
     hearts of the multitudes, the world's prejudices have to
     be conquered step by step, not at a rush.             [L61]

The hand withdraws. On the new step, a light dusting of snow.
The camera holds on it, then lifts along the path, step after
step, into the cloud.
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — DAY

The cloud has passed. Sunlight returns slowly across the desk,
over the cup and THE PIONEER.

Sinnett opens his eyes. Breathes. Reads on.

CONTINUE INTO SCENE 06 (opens on L62).
```

## Visual and staging notes

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Character images** | Sinnett `alfred-percy-sinnett.jpg`, Blavatsky `helena-blavatsky.jpg`, K.H. `koot-hoomi.jpg` (silhouette only: long hair, fur cap, and the fur-trimmed sleeve on the path). The picnic guests, crowds and the people in the boxes have no images and stay as masses, silhouettes, hands and backs |
| **Sinnett's ageing** | Beat 5 starts him younger and ends on his anime portrait. That makes the image's age a story point rather than a problem (decision D4). It needs a younger version of his image (darker beard, more hair) for the start of the arena |
| **The peak** | Give this scene the most screen time and the strongest visual change in the film. It's built as one continuous rise: theatre, amphitheatre, arena, the world outside, the break-in, the shreds, then a hard stop to silence. The scene before (04) is quiet, which helps it land |
| **Escalation rhythm** | Each marvel comes quicker and the applause gets shorter, so the cutting speeds up through beats 3–5 and the music climbs with it. Then the camera rises slowly over the hundreds of millions, a held breath before the break-in |
| **The silhouette** | K.H. behind the curtain is the only glimpse of him here: far above, motionless, out of reach. That's the visual meaning of "invisible operators". Still no face, saving the reveal for scene 08 |
| **Blavatsky in the spotlight** | A single hard white spotlight, everything else black. Sinnett swept into the dark makes clear the danger falls on her. The falling cigarette is her last gesture before the roar |
| **The shreds** | The scene's image to remember: thousands of white scraps of newspaper falling through the spotlight like snow. The tearing should be calm and well-mannered, which is what makes it cruel. It echoes the trampled *Pioneer* in scene 02 |
| **Silence after the peak** | Cut all sound at once. Hold on the empty stage, the falling shreds and the burning cigarette for a long beat. This is "the silence in front of" the resolution |
| **Step by step** | The mountain path answers the arena. The arena is fast, loud and horizontal; the path is slow, silent and vertical. The chisel hand in the fur-trimmed sleeve makes it K.H.'s own work |
| **Palette** | Study: grey under the cloud, warming at the very end. The faces of a million years: firelight, stone grey, ledger brown, gaslight. Arena: red velvet and gold at first, the blue *Akás* flood at the height of wonder, then hard white spotlight on black. Shreds: white on black. Mountain path: blue-white snow light, the calmest image in the film |
| **Text in frame** | No readable newspaper mastheads or dates. *The Pioneer* is recognisable by shape and fold only |
| **Treatment of the crowd** | The letter calls them "the ignorant". The script shows them frightened and angry, not monstrous. Keep faces human: fear as much as rage. The "middle and lower classes" line in L49 is voiced over the faces of a million years, not over any one group |
| **Sound** | Study: silence, a clock. The faces montage: a door, a shutter and a ledger each closing on the beat. Arena: warm applause turning to a roar, a rising musical crescendo, the stage creaking under the noise. The sea of people: a low rumble like an approaching storm. Break-in: wall cracking, the roar. Shreds: paper tearing, again and again, close and crisp over the din. Then total silence. Path: wind, a chisel on stone, falling chips. Study: birdsong returning |
