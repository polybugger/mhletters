# Scene 10 — Your Sincere Friend

| | |
|---|---|
| **Letter lines** | L97–L100 |
| **Settings** | S3 Sinnett's study · S14 the adepts' world (K.H.'s room) |
| **Characters** | Alfred Percy Sinnett, Koot' Hoomi, Helena Petrovna Blavatsky |
| **Feeling** | Quiet resolution |
| **Weight** | S |

## Purpose

- End the letter as it ends: with an invitation, not a door shut. "Try — and first work upon the material you have and then we will be the first to help you". The refusal of scene 02 becomes a promise of help.
- Join the two writers. K.H. writes the last lines and signs in his room, while Sinnett, in his, begins writing at last. Two pens, two desks, the same mountains.
- Close Sinnett's arc (from wanting a miracle to acting on what he has) and pay off scene 01. Blavatsky's "They will" was answered.
- Leave the story resolved and calm. What happened afterwards belongs to scene 11.

**Rules for this scene.** K.H. lines are word for word with line numbers. The short exchange between Sinnett and Blavatsky is [dramatized] and echoes their last words in scene 01. K.H. doesn't speak aloud: his words stay in voice-over.

## Beat outline

1. **"Try —"** Sinnett's pen is poised over the fresh sheet. In his room, K.H. writes the same word.
2. **The promise.** "…then we will be the first to help you to get further evidence." Sinnett lowers his pen to the paper.
3. **The signature.** K.H. writes "always your sincere friend" and signs. Sinnett reads the name aloud to himself.
4. **The pen set down.** K.H. blots the page, folds the letter and lays his pen aside. He looks out at the mountains.
5. **"They did."** Blavatsky in Sinnett's doorway. A callback to scene 01: she said they would answer, and they have.
6. **Two windows.** Sinnett writes his first line. Through his window and through K.H.'s, the same snow peaks. The letter rests on the desk beside the cup.

## Script

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — LATE AFTERNOON

The light has turned gold and low. Long shadows across the
desk: the cup, the cigarette paper, the note, THE PIONEER, the
open letter at its last page.

Sinnett, pen poised over the FRESH SHEET from scene 09. He
hesitates.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     Try —                                                 [L97]
```

```
INT. THE ADEPTS' WORLD — K.H.'S ROOM — EVENING

Lamplight. Snow at the window. The clouded glass of ink from
scene 08 still on the desk, now settled and still.

K.H. at his desk, writing the last lines of the letter. We see
his face, calm and attentive, and the pen forming the word.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …and first work upon the material you have and then we
     will be the first to help you to get further evidence.  [L97]
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — CONTINUOUS

The pen touches Sinnett's page. A first stroke of ink.

He stops, and turns back to the letter for its last lines.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
          (warm, simple)
     Until then, believe me, always your sincere friend,   [L98]
```

```
INT. K.H.'S ROOM — CONTINUOUS

K.H. signs. The pen moves in one unhurried line.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     Koot' Hoomi Lal Singh                                 [L100]
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — CONTINUOUS

Sinnett reads the signature. His lips move.

                    SINNETT
          (quietly, to himself) [dramatized]
     Koot' Hoomi Lal Singh.

He says it like a name he expects to say often.
```

```
INT. K.H.'S ROOM — CONTINUOUS

K.H. blots the page. Lifts it. Folds the letter once, twice,
along creases we have watched Sinnett unfold since scene 02.

He sets down his PEN beside the folded letter, and the glass of
settled ink.

He looks out of the window: snow, the mountains under the
first stars, and far below, the faintest scatter of lights on
a distant ridge.

He rests his hands on the desk, and is still.
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — LATE AFTERNOON

A soft sound at the door. BLAVATSKY in the doorway, as she
stood at the end of scene 01, shawl around her shoulders. No
candle this time; the gold light reaches her.

She looks at the open letter, then at the fresh sheet under his
pen.

                    SINNETT [dramatized]
          (without turning)
     They answered.

                    BLAVATSKY [dramatized]
     I said they would.
          (beat)
     And?

                    SINNETT [dramatized]
          (a small, rueful smile)
     And I am to work with what I have.

She considers him. For the first time in the film, she smiles,
tired and genuine.

                    BLAVATSKY [dramatized]
     Then work.

She goes. This time the doorway is full of light.
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — LATE AFTERNOON

Sinnett turns back to the fresh sheet and begins to WRITE:
steadily now, line after line, the way we first saw him on the
hillside in scene 01, filling the page.

Beside his hand on the desk: the CUP, the CIGARETTE PAPER, the
NOTE, THE PIONEER, and the LETTER, folded closed now, its last
page outward.

The camera rises past him to the WINDOW. Beyond Simla's roofs,
the evening has cleared, and the far SNOW PEAKS of the Himalaya
stand pink and gold in the last sun.
```

```
INT. K.H.'S ROOM — EVENING

The same peaks, from the other side, through K.H.'s window,
under the first stars. K.H. still at his desk, the lamp
burning low.

MATCH CUT between the two windows: the same mountain line in
both, one in sunset gold, one in starlight blue.

HOLD on the peaks.

FADE OUT.

CONTINUE INTO SCENE 11 (CODA).
```

## Visual and staging notes

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Character images** | Sinnett `alfred-percy-sinnett.jpg`, K.H. `koot-hoomi.jpg` (face visible, as since scene 08), Blavatsky `helena-blavatsky.jpg` |
| **Two writers** | The scene's structure: cross-cutting between K.H. finishing the letter and Sinnett starting his own writing, so the end of one letter is the start of Sinnett's next piece of work. Time is folded: K.H. writes the lines as Sinnett reads them. It's storytelling, not a claim about how the letter was sent |
| **The signature** | The voice reads "Koot' Hoomi Lal Singh" as K.H. signs, then Sinnett says the name himself, the only time he speaks it. Keep both readings quiet |
| **The fold** | K.H. folds the letter along the creases Sinnett opened in scene 02, which closes the loop the film began with |
| **Blavatsky's callback** | In scene 01 she left with "They will" and a candle, and the room went dark. Here the doorway fills with light, and she smiles for the first time. Short and warm, without sentiment |
| **The two windows** | The final image: the same Himalayan peaks from Simla (sunset gold) and from K.H.'s room (starlight blue). The two worlds of the film in one match cut. The faint lights on a distant ridge from K.H.'s window suggest Simla far away, without stating it |
| **Recurring props** | Everything from earlier scenes ends on Sinnett's desk: the cup (scene 01), *The Pioneer* (scenes 01–09), the cigarette paper and note (scene 09), and the letter, now folded closed. In K.H.'s room, the glass of ink from scene 08 has settled |
| **Palette** | Study: low gold afternoon light, the warmest the room has been. K.H.'s room: lamplight and starlight blue. The two windows: gold and blue, the film's two colours of the human and the hidden |
| **Pacing** | Short and unhurried. Let the cross-cuts breathe, and hold the final match cut long before the fade |
| **Sound** | Study: a pen nib on paper, the clock, birds settling at dusk. K.H.'s room: lamp hiss, a pen, the soft crackle of paper folding, then silence. Blavatsky's line over the faint sound of the town below. Final shots: wind over snow, one sustained, resolving note into the fade |
