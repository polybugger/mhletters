# Scene 07 — The Natural Shields

| | |
|---|---|
| **Letter lines** | L70–L76 |
| **Settings** | S3 Sinnett's study · S12 modern persecutions (montage): a London courtroom, distant fires, a music hall · a sealed vault (vision) · S13 the temple of Saraswati and Isis |
| **Characters** | Alfred Percy Sinnett, Koot' Hoomi (V.O., one passing glimpse). Vision figures, silent: a medium, a magistrate, villagers, a stage conjurer, Saraswati, Isis, disciples |
| **Feeling** | Sober, then hushed |
| **Weight** | M |

## Purpose

- Bring the gallery's history into the present. Mediums are prosecuted and "supposed witches" burned in 1880, so "old history" isn't old.
- Deliver the letter's twist: the frauds protect the real thing. While the public laughs at "charlatans and jugglers", the genuine adepts pass unnoticed.
- Explain the secrecy one last way. The adepts' knowledge could be "terrible weapons" in the wrong hands.
- Turn from danger to devotion. Phenomena are a reward for the faithful, not a show for the "profanes". This closes the letter's main argument before its personal, practical ending (scenes 08–10).

**Rules for this scene.** K.H. lines are word for word with line numbers, and "…" marks a cut. Vision figures are silent. The persecutions are shown at a distance: a courtroom, fires on a horizon, never violence close up. Sinnett is silent. The letter's claims about the adepts are staged as claims.

## Beat outline

1. **Old history?** Sinnett glances back at the empty space on his wall. "All this is old history, you will think."
2. **The dock.** A London courtroom today. A medium in the dock, a magistrate, a packed and jeering gallery.
3. **The fires.** Three horizons at night (South America, Russia, the frontiers of Spain), each with a fire and torches against the dark.
4. **The natural shields.** A music hall. A conjurer produces a dove from a hat, and the audience laughs and cheers: it's all tricks. Through the crowd, unnoticed, walks a figure in a fur cap.
5. **The terrible weapons.** A heavy door in a mountain monastery. A hand in a fur-trimmed sleeve turns a key. Behind it, light pulses like something held in check.
6. **The reward.** The temple: Saraswati with her veena, then Isis, veiled, behind her as one presence. A devoted disciple receives a small blue light in cupped hands.
7. **The profanes.** At the temple gate, people peer in, held back. Among them, hat in hand, is a younger Sinnett.
8. **Back to the study.** Sinnett lowers the letter. The argument is over, and the personal part begins.

## Script

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — DAY

Sinnett glances up again at the empty space on his wall from
scene 06. A faint, rueful smile: the past is the past.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
          (anticipating him)
     All this is old history, you will think.              [L70]

His smile fades.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     Verily so; but the chronicles of our modern days do not
     differ very essentially from their predecessors.      [L71]

On his desk, beside THE PIONEER: a London paper, weeks old,
folded at a court report. He pulls it toward him.
```

```
INT. A LONDON POLICE COURT — DAY

Brown wood, gaslight, a high bench. A MAGISTRATE in a wig.
Constables. A GALLERY packed with spectators in hats and
bonnets, craning, whispering, laughing.

In the dock: a MEDIUM, a thin, respectable man in a black
coat, a slate under a constable's arm as evidence. He stares
straight ahead while the gallery sniggers.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     And we have but to bear in mind the recent persecutions
     of mediums in England,                                [L72]

The magistrate's gavel comes down.
```

```
EXT. THREE HORIZONS — NIGHT (MONTAGE)

Three wide, distant shots, the same composition each time: a
dark landscape, a fire on the horizon, the silhouettes of
villagers with torches gathered round it.

— Mountains and forest. SOUTH AMERICA.
— A flat snowy plain, a wooden village. RUSSIA.
— A dry hillside, a stone church tower. THE FRONTIERS OF SPAIN.

We never come close. Only the fire, the torches, the crowd.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …the burning of supposed witches, and sorcerers in
     South America, Russia and the frontiers of Spain —    [L72]
```

```
INT. A MUSIC HALL — NIGHT

A sudden change of tone. Bright footlights, red plush, smoke,
a brass band. A packed, cheerful HOUSE.

On stage, a CONJURER in tails and a moustache, all flourish.
He shows an empty top hat, taps it with his wand and pulls out
a white DOVE. The dove flutters up. The house roars with
laughter and applause.

A MAN in the stalls nudges his wife, delighted: he knows how
it's done. Everyone knows. It's all tricks.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …to assure ourselves that the only salvation of the
     genuine proficients in occult sciences lies in the
     skepticism of the public:                             [L72]

Down the side aisle, through the laughing crowd, walks a
FIGURE in a dark fur-trimmed robe and FUR CAP, long dark hair
over his shoulders. Unhurried. Nobody turns to look. We see
him only from behind, half lost in smoke.

He passes the end of a row. A child glances at him, curious.
Her mother's hand turns her back to the stage.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     …the charlatans and the jugglers are the natural shields
     of the "adepts."                                      [L72]

The figure reaches the back of the hall, opens the door to the
night and is gone. On stage, the conjurer bows.
```

```
INT. A MOUNTAIN MONASTERY — A VAULTED PASSAGE — NIGHT

Snow light through a slit window. Stone. Silence.

A great DOOR of dark wood and iron, ancient, scarred.

Through the cracks round its edge, a LIGHT pulses: white-blue,
strong, slow, like something powerful breathing behind it.

The HAND in the fur-trimmed sleeve turns a heavy KEY in the
lock. The bolt shoots home. The light behind the door steadies,
dims and holds.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     The public safety is only ensured by our keeping secret
     the terrible weapons which might otherwise be used
     against it, and which, as you have been told became
     deadly in the hands of the wicked and selfish.        [L73]

The hand withdraws. The key stays in the lock.
```

```
INT. THE TEMPLE OF SARASWATI — DAWN

Hush. A different world. A pillared temple open to the dawn,
lamps burning low, a lotus pool, a white SWAN gliding on the
water.

On a lotus seat, SARASWATI: in white, serene, a VEENA in her
arms, her fingers resting on its strings.

Behind her, so close she seems part of the same presence,
taller and veiled: ISIS, winged, a throne crown on her head,
her wings folding gently around the scene like an embrace.
Two goddesses from two lands, one figure: "our Aryan Isis."

                    K.H. (V.O.)
          (softer than we have heard him)
     I conclude by reminding you that such phenomena as you
     crave, have ever been reserved as a reward for those
     who have devoted their lives to serve the goddess
     Saraswati — our Aryan Isis.                           [L75]

At the foot of the lotus seat kneel DISCIPLES in plain robes.
Old and young. One, grey-haired, has knelt there a long time;
we can tell by the worn stone under his knees.

He lifts his cupped hands. In them, softly, a small BLUE LIGHT
appears, the Akás glow, the size of a candle flame. He bows
his head over it, utterly still.
```

```
EXT. THE TEMPLE GATE — DAWN

At the outer GATE, a low carved wall. Beyond it, peering in:
PEOPLE from the outside world, the music-hall crowd, gentlemen
in hats and ladies with parasols. Curious, impatient, held
back by nothing but the threshold.

Among them, hat in hand, a YOUNGER SINNETT (as at the start of
the arena in scene 05). He leans forward, straining to see the
light in the disciple's hands.

                    K.H. (V.O.)
     Were they given to the profanes what would remain for
     our faithful ones?                                    [L76]

The younger Sinnett looks down at the threshold under his
boots. He does not step over it.
```

```
INT. SINNETT'S STUDY — DAY

Sinnett lowers the letter. A long breath.

He looks at the dug-up CUP on the desk, his own small
phenomenon, now ordinary in the daylight.

He turns to the last pages.

CONTINUE INTO SCENE 08 (opens on L77).
```

| **The persecutions** | Keep them distant and quiet. The courtroom is about humiliation (the sniggering gallery), not drama. The fires are three far-off silhouettes, never people burning. The letter's point is that these things happen, not their spectacle |
| **The twist** | The music hall is the scene's key image: a loud, laughing room where everyone is sure it's all tricks, and the real adept walks through unseen. The child who notices him, turned back by her mother's hand, is the scene's quiet human moment |
| **K.H.'s glimpses** | Still no face. Here he's a figure from behind in the music hall, and the hand on the key in the monastery, the same sleeve as scenes 02 and 05. His face is saved for scene 08 |
| **The weapons** | Never show what the "terrible weapons" are. Only light pulsing behind a locked door. The hand locking it, and the key left in the lock, are enough |
| **Saraswati and Isis** | One composite presence, not two separate statues: Saraswati in front, Isis's wings around her. That makes "our Aryan Isis" a single image. Keep it devotional and beautiful, the calmest place in the film after the mountain path |
| **The disciple's light** | The blue *Akás* colour from scenes 03, 05 and 06 returns as something given freely and small. The same force that flooded the arena and was dimmed in the gallery fits in two cupped hands here |
| **Sinnett at the gate** | The younger Sinnett among the "profanes" places him outside without judging him. He doesn't step over the threshold. It's a gentle moment, not a rebuke, and leads into the warmer scenes that follow |
| **Palette** | Study: plain daylight. Courtroom: brown wood and dull gaslight. Fires: black landscapes with one orange point each. Music hall: hot red, gold and smoke. Monastery: blue-white snow light and black stone, the door's light white-blue. Temple: dawn rose and gold, white for Saraswati, deep blue and gold for Isis's wings, the disciple's blue light. Gate: the same dawn, the visitors in dark Victorian clothes against it |
| **Text in frame** | The court report in the London paper is shown by layout only, not readable. No place-name captions on the three horizons. Landscape and architecture identify them |
| **Pacing** | The montage (courtroom, fires) moves briskly. The music hall slows for the walking figure. The monastery is held still. The temple is the slowest passage of the scene. Let the hush land before scene 08 |
| **Sound** | Study: a clock, a newspaper unfolding. Courtroom: murmurs, sniggers, the gavel. Fires: wind, far-off voices and the crackle of fire, very low.
