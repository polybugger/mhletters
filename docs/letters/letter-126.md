# Letter No. 126

*{P.S. to Letter 5; written around November 26, 1880}*

P.S. It is exceedingly difficult to make arrangements for a Punjab address through which to correspond.
Both B. and I had counted much upon the young man whose sentimentalism we find unfits him for the useful office of intermediary.
Still, I will not cease trying and shall hope to send you the name of a post office either in the Punjab or N.W.P. where one of our friends will be passing and repassing once or twice a month.
K. H.
