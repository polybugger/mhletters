# Letter No. 143

*{Received Allahabad, October 27, 1880}*

Would you wish the pillow phenomenon described in the paper?
I will

gladly follow your advice.
Ever yours, A. P.Sinnett

It certainly would be the best thing to do, and I personally would feel sincerely thankful to you on account of our much ill-used friend.
You are at liberty to mention my first name if it will in the least help you.

Koot Hoomi Lal Sing
