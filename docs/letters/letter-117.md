# Letter No. 117

*{Received Simla, mid-October, 1882)*

This will introduce to my Chela (lay) No. 1 “Lay Chela No. 2” — Mohini Babu.
The experiences of the latter and what he has to say will interest Mr. Sinnett.
Mohini Babu is sent by me on a certain mission in reference to the forthcoming and very threatening end of the cycle (theosophical) — and has no time to lose.
Please, receive him at once and take his evidence.

Yours,

K. H.
