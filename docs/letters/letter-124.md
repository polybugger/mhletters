# Letter No. 124

*{Received Madras, late March, 1883}*

Cannot you manage to pick up for me three pebbles?
They must come from the shores of the Adriatic — Venice preferably; as near to the Dogal Palace as they can possibly be found; (under the Bridge of Sighs would be the most desirable but for the mud of the ages).
The pebbles must be of three different colours one — red, the other black; the third white (or greyish).
If you manage to get them, please keep them apart from every influence and contact but yours, and oblige ever yours,

K. H.
