# Letter No. 101

*Received Simla, {October} 1881.*

Your letter received.
I believe you had better try and see whether you could not make your ideas less polemical and dry than his.
I begin to think there may be some stuff in you, since you are able so to appreciate my beloved friend and brother.
I have attended to the Brahmin boy’s letter and erased the offensive sentence replacing it with another.
You can now show it to the Maha Sahib; him so proud in his *bakbak* humility and so humble in his pride.
As for phenomena you will have none — I have written through Olcott.
Blessed is he who knows our Koothoomi and blessed is he who appreciates him.
What I now mean you will understand some day.
As for your A.O.H. I *know him better* than you ever will.

M⸫
