# Letter No. 142b

Respectfully submitted for the consideration of Mr. Sinnett, under the direct orders of Brother Koot Hoomi.

Damodar K. Mavalankar

With the exception of fee — too exaggerated — his views are quite correct.
Such is the impression produced upon the native mind.
I trust, my dear friend, that you add a paragraph showing the Society in its true light.
Listen to your *inner voice*, and oblige once more your’s

Ever faithfully, K. H.
