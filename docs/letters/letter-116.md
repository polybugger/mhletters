# Letter No. 116

*{Received Simla, early October 1881}*

*A. P. Sinnett*

My dear Friend, I am tired and disgusted with all this wrangling to death.
Please read this before giving it to Mr. Hume.
If, as a debt of gratitude, he would exact but a pound of flesh, I would have naught to say — but a pound of useless verbiage is indeed more than even I — can stand!

Yours ever,

K. H.
