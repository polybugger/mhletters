# Letter No. 121

*Received at Bombay on return to India, July {11}, 1881.*

Thanks.
The little things prove very useful, and I gratefully acknowledge them.
You ought to go to Simla.
Try.
I confess to a weakness on my part to see you do so.
We must patiently await the results, as I told you of *the* Book.
The *blanks* are provoking and “tantalizing” but we cannot go against the inevitable.
And as it is always good to mend an error I already did so by presenting the *Occult World* to the C—’s notice.
Patience, patience.

Yours, ever

K. H.
