# Letter No. 120

*{Received Allahabad, March 11, 1882}*

To Mr. Sinnett’s “Lady.”

Wear the hair enclosed in a *cotton* tape (and if preferred in a metal armlet) a little lower than your *left* armpit below the left shoulder.
Follow advice that will be given to you by Henry Olcott.
It is good and we shall not object.
Harbour not ill-feelings even against an enemy and one who has wronged you: for hatred acts like an antidote and may damage the effect of even *this hair.*

K. H.
