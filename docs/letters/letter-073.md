# Letter No. 73

*{Received Simla, October, 1881}*

Mr. Sinnett — you will receive a long letter — posted Sunday at Bombay — from the Brahmin boy.
Koot-hoomi went to see him (as he is his *Chela*) before going into “Tong-pa-ngi” — the state in which he now is — and left with him certain orders.
The boy has a little bungled up the message so be very careful before you show it to Mr. Hume lest he should again misunderstand my Brother’s real meaning.
I will *not* stand any more nonsense, or bad feeling against him, but retire at once.

We do the best we can.
M⸫
